Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

ExpenseFlow 360 is built on an offline-first architecture designed to keep your financial life private. This policy explains what data stays on your device, how optional services operate, and your privacy rights.

1. Offline-First Financial Data Storage

  • All accounts, wallets, income/expense transactions, transfers, budgets, savings goals, scheduled bills, debt records, and categories are stored locally on your device using Android Room SQLite and Preferences DataStore.
  • Tech Nest Studio does not require account registration and does not collect, upload, view, sell, or back up your personal financial ledger to any remote servers.

2. App Lock & Cryptographic PIN Protection

  • Enabling the 4-digit PIN Lock protects access to the app on your device.
  • Your PIN is never stored in plain text; it is combined with a local salt and hashed on-device before persistence.
  • Because no data is sent to a server, forgetting your PIN cannot be reset remotely and may require clearing app storage.

3. PDF Financial Reports & Exports

  • PDF financial summaries are rendered locally on your device using Android's PdfDocument engine.
  • Exported files are written only to the folder you pick via Storage Access Framework or shared temporarily via FileProvider when you tap Share.

4. Advertising & Google UMP Privacy Consent

  • The Free tier displays frequency-capped advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Where required by regional regulations (such as EEA/UK/US states), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests your consent before loading personalized or non-personalized ads, and you can update preferences anytime in Settings.
  • Upgrading to ExpenseFlow 360 Premium completely disables all ad requests and removes every ad placement across the app.

5. Google Play Billing & Subscriptions

  • Monthly, 6-Month, and Yearly Premium subscriptions are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing.
  • Tech Nest Studio never accesses or stores your credit card, bank account, or billing address details—only non-sensitive entitlement status tokens returned by Google Play.

6. Device Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely for Google Play Billing entitlement checks, AdMob ad serving (Free tier), and UMP privacy consent.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional, Android 13+): Requested only if you turn on local Budget Threshold Alerts or Daily Logging Reminders, which run locally via Android WorkManager.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or broad storage permissions are requested.

7. Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

  • ExpenseFlow 360 is a general personal and small-business finance tool and is not intended for children under 13 years of age.
  • We may update this Privacy Policy when new features or regulatory requirements arise. Updated versions are published directly inside the app and on this page.
